How the odds work

We read the National Weather Service forecast for your district and the weather of the past three days, then compare overnight snow, ice and wind chill with how much snow your area usually gets (NOAA 1991–2020 normals). Places that rarely see snow close for less. "Was school closed?" answers from users tune the odds for each district over time.

The chance is an estimate. Your district makes the call — always check its official announcement. We are not affiliated with any school district.

Season Pass

One payment covers the snow season it's bought in (through June 30). It never renews. To restore it on a new device, open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Frequently asked questions

Why does it say 1% when it's snowing?

The chance is about the next school day and compares the forecast with what your area usually handles. A few inches in Buffalo is a normal day; the same snow in Atlanta can close schools.

My district closed but the app said it wouldn't.

Districts sometimes close for reasons the weather can't show, such as bus problems or cleanup after a storm. Tap "Was school closed?" in the app — your answer helps the odds for your district learn its habits.

Why can't I find my district?

Search by district name, city, or 5-digit ZIP code. The list comes from the National Center for Education Statistics (2024–25). If yours is missing, email us.

I don't get alerts.

Alerts need the Season Pass and notification permission. iOS decides exactly when the app may check in the background, so alerts can arrive later than expected — opening the app always shows the latest odds.

How do I delete my answers?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete My Answers.
