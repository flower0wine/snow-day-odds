Privacy Policy

Snow Day Odds · Last updated October 17, 2026

Snow Day Odds estimates the chance that school will be closed for snow. We built it to work without accounts, ads, or tracking.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There is no sign-up, no advertising SDK, and no analytics SDK.

DataWhere it goesWhy
The school district you pickStays on your device. Its map coordinates are sent to the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to fetch the forecast.To make the prediction
Your location (only if you tap "Find districts near me")Used once, on your device, to sort districts by distance. Never stored or sent anywhere.To list nearby districts
"Was school closed?" answers (only if you answer)Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database): district ID, date, your answer, and the chance we showed. Stored with a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, or Apple Account details.To make predictions more accurate for each district
PurchasesHandled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.Season Pass

Notifications are scheduled on your device. We do not run a server that knows who you are.

Children

Snow Day Odds is meant for users 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third parties

  • National Weather Service receives the coordinates of the district you choose (public-domain weather data).
  • Apple processes purchases and stores anonymous "Was school closed?" answers in iCloud.

We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Your choices

  • Don't want to share answers? Simply don't answer — the app works the same.
  • Location and notifications can be turned off anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
